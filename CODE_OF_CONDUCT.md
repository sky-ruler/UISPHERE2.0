# GDG Community Code of Conduct

## Tekno Krraft 2026 &mdash; Google Developer Group @ SOA, ITER Chapter

### Our Pledge
**Tekno Krraft 2026**, organized by the Google Developer Group (GDG) at Siksha 'O' Anusandhan (SOA), ITER Chapter, is dedicated to providing a harassment-free and inclusive event and learning experience for everyone, regardless of gender, sexual orientation, disability, physical appearance, body size, race, or religion.

### Community Standards
- **Be Welcoming & Inclusive:** Encourage diversity of thought, background, and experience. Support beginner developers and students.
- **Collaborative Spirit:** Focus on constructive feedback, ethical coding practices, open source sharing, and mutual assistance during hack sprints and technical workshops.
- **Respect Boundaries:** Treat all attendees, student volunteers, judges, mentors, and speakers with kindness and dignity.
- **Zero Tolerance for Harassment:** Harassment, offensive verbal remarks, intimidation, sustained disruption of talks or labs, and inappropriate imagery will result in immediate disqualification and removal from the event.

### Reporting & Enforcement
If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, or feel unsafe at any point during Tekno Krraft 2026:
- Approach any student organizer or volunteer wearing official GDG credentials.
- Contact the organizing leads directly via email at **[gdg@iter.ac.in](mailto:gdg@iter.ac.in)**.
- All reports will be handled promptly with confidentiality.
